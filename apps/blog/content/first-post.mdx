# Hello, Pink Binder Blog

Welcome to the very first post on the **Pink Binder** blog! 🎉

If you found your way here, chances are you share our obsession with the pretty, pastel, and art-heavy side of the Pokémon TCG universe. Whether you're here to find the perfect addition to your cozy card binder, watch our live break streams, or take a look behind the curtain at the custom code powering this entire ecosystem, we're incredibly excited to have you.

This blog is going to be a dual-purpose space. Half the time, we’ll be talking about card hunting, imports, and community updates. The other half, we'll dive straight into the engineering side—sharing how a single developer can use AI to build a massive, automated multi-channel storefront from scratch.

---

## What is Pink Binder?

Pink Binder started out of a simple frustration. When you look at major trading card marketplaces, everything feels optimized for competitive players or high-end financial investors tracking charts.

But what about the collectors who just want to collect beautiful art? The ones who organize their collections by color gradients? The fans hunting down the absolute cutest Pokémon in existence?

We realized that "cute" is its own massive, highly passionate market. We wanted to build a dedicated home for that community. Pink Binder is a curated ecosystem specializing exclusively in:

- **The Pastel & Fairy Aesthetic:** Sylveon, Jigglypuff, Clefairy, and the dreamiest card arts out there.
- **Chibi & Tiny Favorites:** Adorable baby shinies, round friends like Rowlet, and modern favorites like the Maushold family.
- **Global Art Across Borders:** We hunt down authentic English, Japanese, and Chinese singles—especially the hard-to-find Art Rares (AR) and Special Illustration Rares (SIR)—so you don't have to navigate confusing foreign listing boards.

---

## The Tech Behind the Magic

To pull this off without paying thousands of dollars a month in bloated platform fees, we are building everything completely in-house. As a solo developer collaborating with cutting-edge AI via Cursor, the entire digital footprint of Pink Binder runs inside a single code repository known as a **Turborepo**.

By running a monorepo, our system is split into specialized micro-apps that share the same DNA:

- **`apps/landing`:** A high-performance, lightning-fast Next.js 15 front page optimized heavily for search engines.
- **`apps/blog`:** This exact space you are reading right now, built entirely on lightweight MDX files so it costs $0 to host.
- **`apps/store`:** Our clean storefront where you can buy singles directly without the clunky interface of traditional marketplaces.
- **`apps/admin`:** Our brain center. A custom-built content and inventory management dashboard hooked up to a live Supabase database.

### The Automated Sync Engine

The coolest piece of tech we’re building is our real-time inventory sync. Since we stream live and list cards across multiple platforms (like eBay and Etsy), manually updating quantities when a card sells is a nightmare.

We’re writing custom webhook handlers and browser automation scripts that act as a single source of truth. When someone buys an Illustration Rare on our live stream or our website, our backend pushes an instant update to wipe that stock across every other platform simultaneously. No accidental double sales, no manual spreadsheet tracking.
